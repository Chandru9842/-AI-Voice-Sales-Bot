🎓 Platform Features & Verification Checklist

1. Voice Conversation & Synthesis

Bot speaks with realistic speech synthesis, listens via continuous speech recognition, detects interruptions if the customer speaks, and pitches the SaaS product naturally.

2. Language Support

Supports English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Telugu (తెలుగు) for both voice recognition and speech synthesis output, with localized sales scripts.

3. Real-Time Live Chat Screen

Live chat stream presents bot messages and customer speech in real time with speaker avatars, exact call timestamps, and audio waveform equalizer animation.

4. Call Controls, Duration & Status

Provides Start Call, End Call, Mute Microphone, Speaker Mute, live duration timer (00:00), and real-time status transitions (Ready, Speaking, Listening, Ended, Analyzing).

5. Call Recording & Audio Playback with Consent

Mandatory recording consent dialog before call begins. Captures audio via MediaRecorder, saves audio to server and local storage, and provides interactive audio playback with download option.

6. AI Summary, Requirements, Objections & Next Steps

Automatically generates executive summary, extracted customer requirements, objections raised and handled, and recommended next steps immediately after every call.

7. Automated Sales Status Assignment

Accurately classifies calls into the 6 required statuses: Converted, Interested, Follow-up Required, Not Interested, Demo Requested, and No Response.

8. Call History & CRM

Comprehensive CRM dashboard with KPI metrics, status filters, search, language filtering, deep-dive dialogue inspection, recording playback, and JSON / PDF export.
